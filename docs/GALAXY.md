# Galaxy boundary

A.T.L.A.S. is one independent galaxy:

> one repository, one Page, one complete runtime, one tested contract

Its core behavior does not require Battle/Star.SOL, xCommand, GZG:NOW, a
wallet, an account, a game server, or a remote asset host. It starts with a
local textured globe over HTTP and a procedural globe when opened directly as
a file.

## Owned here

- strategic Earth rendering and camera controls;
- eight display profiles and 18 layer switches;
- built-in local demonstration data;
- bounded feed ingestion and optional detection-radius display filtering;
- coordinate and event inspection;
- snapshot URL state;
- normalized deployment-selection output; and
- the generic A.T.L.A.S. visual and I/O reference.

## Owned elsewhere

- tactical rules and resolution belong to xCommand;
- Battle/Star narrative, factions, and economy belong to Battle/Star.SOL;
- identity, evidence, and settlement belong to their named protocol galaxies;
  and
- a composed launcher owns the adapter between its strategic and tactical
  surfaces.

No consumer may require a mutable copy of this repository at runtime. A
consumer copies or version-pins the contract and either embeds a release or
opens this Page with an explicit message adapter.

## Standalone fallback

With all peers offline a user can still:

1. open the Page;
2. inspect the globe and built-in layers;
3. click any Earth coordinate;
4. download the resulting versioned selection message; and
5. preserve or share the current view as a snapshot URL.

The Page labels local simulation and optional feeds honestly. It does not turn
missing network authority into a simulated claim.
